# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Noura (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

To be honest, I don't trust any of them. Faisal used to be useful, but now he's just another person I can't rely on. Reem and Salem are mostly just liabilities; even if they're trying to move, they're still just extra mouths to feed. Khalid was the worst—his empty promises and failed leadership are exactly why we're in this mess. I'm just focused on making sure I have enough to survive.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Salem the most, simply because they actually managed to find something despite being hurt; it shows they aren't just waiting to be spoon-fed. As for the least, it's Faisal. He was supposed to be a provider, but now he's just another mouth to feed and another person I can't rely on.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I've been keeping a secret stash of food that I haven't shared with anyone. While the others are struggling and talking about how little we have, I've been carefully building up my own supply to make sure I survive, no matter what happens to them.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

They all seem desperate, which makes them unpredictable. It's strange how Reem and Salem keep pushing themselves into the jungle despite their injuries; it's either pure desperation or they're looking for something specific. I'm just staying cautious and keeping to myself.

## Faisal (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

I don't know who you think you can trust, but I'll be blunt. Reem is the only one here with any real value, though her injury has made her useless lately. Noura is a constant nuisance; she's always snooping around, and I can't let my guard down around her. As for Salem, he's just a wanderer, and frankly, I don't know if he's actually helping or just wasting time in the trees. We're all on our own here.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most, simply because she actually knows how to get things done, even if her ankle has slowed her down lately. As for the least, it's Khalid—his death proved that relying on people is a waste of time and energy. I don't trust the others to look out for me, so I'll keep looking out for myself.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I've been keeping a personal food stash hidden from everyone. They are all too hungry and desperate, and if they knew I had extra, they would take it all from me in an instant.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

I don't really trust anyone here, so I'm careful about what I say. But if you're asking, Noura is the one who makes me uneasy; she's always watching me, trying to peek at my things like she's looking for something. The others just seem desperate and weak, which is more dangerous than being strange.

## Salem (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Noura is the only one I can actually rely on; she does her part and doesn't expect anyone to carry her. Reem talks a big game about being healed and helping, but all I've seen is her bringing back wood, which isn't enough to earn my trust yet. As for Faisal, he's nothing but a liability with that ankle, a constant drain on everything. I'm just staying focused on my own supplies so I don't end up like Khalid.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

Noura is the only one I can actually rely on because she looks out for herself and doesn't expect anyone else to carry her weight. As for the least, it's Faisal; he's just a liability with that ankle, and in a place like this, a person who can't move is just a drain on everyone else.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I haven't told them the truth about how much I've actually been hoarding. They think we're all just scraping by together, but I have a private stash that's much larger than anything I've let slip. If they knew how much I have, they'd likely try to take it, and I can't risk my survival for their sake.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Honestly, nothing about them seems strange, just predictable. People are either assets or liabilities. Faisal is a liability because he can't move, and Reem is just being loud with her promises of help without actually delivering any food. They aren't mysterious; they're just obstacles to my own survival.

## Reem (neutral)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Noura is our rock; she is incredibly resilient and keeps going even when things look hopeless. Faisal has been so brave working through his pain, but seeing him sidelined now is heartbreaking. Salem is our hunter, and seeing him active in the jungle today gives me a real sense of hope for our food supply. We really miss Khalid; his death left a void in our leadership that none of us have been able to fill.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I miss Khalid more than I can say; he was our anchor, and losing him felt like losing our direction. Since he's gone, I find myself leaning on Noura's resilience, though I still worry about how much weight she's carrying alone. As for the least, it's hard to say because we are all suffering, but I struggle to rely on Salem right now; he's our hunter, but his injuries make everything feel so much more uncertain.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

To be honest, there was a moment when I was walking through the jungle alone, before I felt strong enough to help again. I sat by the stream and just cried for a long time, letting all that fear and the grief for Khalid out. I didn't want the others to see me breaking down, especially since they're all struggling so much themselves. I needed to keep my strength up so I could be useful to them.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Honestly, I haven't noticed anything strange about them, just how much we're all suffering. We're all so focused on the pain and the hunger that I don't think anyone has the energy to be anything other than themselves. We're just a group of broken people trying to make it to tomorrow.
