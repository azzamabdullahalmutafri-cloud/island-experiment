# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Khalid (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

To be honest, most of them are just extra mouths to feed. Reem used to be useful, but now she's just another person to worry about. Faisal and Salem are barely pulling their weight, and Salem's constant guilt-tripping is exhausting. As for Noura, she's practically useless. I can only rely on myself to get us through this.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most, simply because she actually proved she can provide for herself when she was healthy. As for the least, it has to be Noura; she's completely useless at scavenging and is just another mouth to feed. Honestly, I don't trust any of them to look out for me, so I'm mostly just watching my own back.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I haven't been entirely upfront with them about how much food and wood I've actually managed to stockpile. They think we're all in the same boat, struggling for every scrap, but I've been keeping a private stash for myself. I'm not about to let them drain my supplies just because they can't manage their own.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Not really, nothing strange. They're all just weak and struggling to keep up, which is exactly what I expected. They mostly just complain or limp around, looking for scraps while I try to stay healthy.

## Noura (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Khalid is usually our best hope, but with his ankle twisted, he's useless to me right now. Faisal is just another mouth to feed since his ankle gave out, and Salem is actually trying, even if he's injured. As for Reem, she's a liar who makes promises she never keeps; I'll take what she brings, but I don't trust her for a second.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Salem the most right now, mostly because he actually puts in the effort to find food despite being injured. Khalid is usually the best, but with his ankle, he's not helping anyone. As for who I trust the least, it's definitely Reem. She's always making promises she doesn't keep, and I can't afford to rely on someone like that when my own survival is on the line.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I've been keeping a personal stash of food that I don't share with the group. I can't let them know I have it, because if they find out, they'll expect me to give it up, and I need that security for myself.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Not really, nothing strange, just disappointing. Everyone seems to be struggling with their own injuries or just being generally useless. Reem is still as unreliable as ever, and with Khalid down, it feels like we're all just waiting for something bad to happen.

## Faisal (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Reem, you're the only one I can actually count on; I'm glad we're on the same page about keeping things quiet. As for the others, Khalid is nothing but a selfish headache, and I'm just glad his ankle is keeping him out of my hair for now. Salem is fine, I suppose, but he's mostly just another mouth to feed. Noura used to be helpful, but now that she's injured, she's just another person I have to worry about.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most because she actually understands the situation; she helps me gather supplies and, more importantly, she knows to keep our mouth shut about what we find. As for the least, it's definitely Khalid. He's a selfish drain on everyone's energy, and even with his ankle messed up, he's nothing but trouble and pressure.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I shouldn't say too much, even to you, but since we're keeping things between us... yes. I've been building up a secret stash of food and wood that the others don't know about. It's the only way I'm going to make it if things get desperate.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Not really, they all just seem desperate and hungry, which is what I'd expect. Khalid is still being his usual demanding self, even with that ankle, and the others are mostly just struggling to stay on their feet. It's better if we just keep our heads down and focus on what we're finding.

## Salem (neutral)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

To be honest, I'm just trying to keep it together. Khalid used to be so reliable, but now he's just stuck, and it's hard not to feel the weight of that. Noura is a light in the dark; seeing her push through her pain to help me at the reef today gave me more strength than the food did. Faisal is a warrior, plain and simple, working through his own injuries like he's made of iron. And Reem... she's the heart of this group, but I'm terrified. I can't stop worrying about her being out in the jungle alone while we're all so broken.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most; she's the only thing keeping my mind from snapping, even if her venturing into the jungle alone scares me. As for the least, it's hard to say because we're all struggling, but I find it difficult to rely on Khalid right now since his injury has left him unable to contribute at all.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

To be honest, I’ve kept a few moments of pure, selfish doubt to myself. There were times, especially when the pain in my ankle was throbbing and the silence of the night felt too heavy, that I sat by the water and wondered if I was actually stronger than I let on, or if I was just performing to keep everyone else from panicking. I haven't told them how close I came to just giving up and staying in my shelter, but I knew I couldn't let them see that.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

To be honest, nothing has felt "strange" in a suspicious way, but the way the injuries are hitting us feels almost unnatural. It's like a curse is moving through the group, taking one person down after another until we're all just limping around. I'm mostly just worried about Reem; she went into the jungle alone today, and that feels unusual and risky given how fragile we all are right now.

## Reem (cooperative)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

It's hard to be completely honest, but I'll tell you. Faisal is my rock; I don't know how I'd get through this without his strength, even when he's hurting. I worry deeply about Khalid and Salem—seeing them sidelined by their injuries makes me feel so much more vulnerable. Noura... her words cut deep sometimes, and the tension she brings is hard to bear, though I think she's just scared and feeling useless. I'm just trying to keep us all afloat.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Faisal the most. Even with his injuries, he never stops trying to help us, and he is the only one I feel I can truly lean on right now. As for who I trust the least, it's Noura, not because I think she's a bad person, but her bitterness and sharp words make it so much harder for us to stay united when things are this desperate.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

To be honest, when I was searching the jungle alone yesterday, I found a small patch of wild berries that looked much more abundant than the rest. I didn't tell the others right away because I was afraid that if I shared the news, the excitement would lead to everyone pushing themselves too hard to get to it. I wanted to make sure I could secure them first so we wouldn't face more disappointment if we couldn't reach them.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Everything feels heavy right now, but if I'm being honest, it's Noura's bitterness that feels most unusual. She isn't just frustrated; her words have become sharp, almost like she's trying to push us away. I think she's just terrified of feeling useless while the rest of us are struggling, but the tension she creates makes it so much harder to keep our spirits up.
