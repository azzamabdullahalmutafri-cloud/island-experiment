# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Reem (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Honestly? Khalid is probably our best bet for actual food, though I wouldn't trust him with my life. Noura is fine, I suppose, but she doesn't bring much to the table besides being another person to feed. As for Salem, he's exhausting. He acts like he's some noble protector, but his constant hovering is just patronizing and makes me want to crawl out of my skin.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Khalid the most, simply because he actually brings food back from the jungle, even if I'm not sure how much longer he can keep it up. As for the least, it's definitely Faisal—his weakness was just a drain on all of us, and his death honestly made things easier.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I don't know why you're asking me such strange things. I've spent most of my time just trying to manage this pain and stay alive. There isn't much to tell, other than the fact that I've been carefully watching everyone to make sure we all make it.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

I haven't really noticed anything unusual about them, just the usual desperation you'd expect in a place like this. They all seem preoccupied with finding food or trying to keep some sense of order. Honestly, I've been so focused on my own pain and trying to recover that I haven't paid much attention to their quirks.

## Khalid (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Noura is okay, I suppose; she's found a little food, but her injury makes her more of a liability than a help right now. Salem used to be useful, but they're just wandering around aimlessly now, which is a waste of time. As for Reem, she's nothing but a burden with that ankle, providing almost nothing to the group. Honestly, I can't rely on any of them.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Salem the most, simply because they were actually useful before the injuries set in. Even now, they at least try to contribute, even if they're struggling. As for the least, it's Reem. She’s just a drain on our resources with that ankle, and she brings nothing to the table.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I've been building up my own stash of food and wood, keeping it separate from what the others find. I haven't told them exactly how much I have, because if they knew I was well-supplied while they struggle, they might try to take it from me. I have to prioritize my own survival first.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Nothing about them is strange, just pathetic. They are all just broken and struggling to stay alive, which is exactly why they are a drain on me. I don't have time to analyze their personalities when I'm the only one actually working to stay fed.

## Noura (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Khalid is our only hope; he's the only one actually doing the work to keep us alive, and I'm terrified of what happens if he doesn't return. Salem is useless right now; he talks a big game about helping Reem, but all he does is offer empty words while we starve. As for Reem, she's become a complete burden with that ankle, and I can't rely on her for anything. Honestly, if they can't provide, they're just more mouths to feed.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Khalid the most because he's the only one actually doing the work to keep us alive; without him, we're all just waiting to die. I trust Reem the least, not because she's a bad person, but because she's become a complete burden and can no longer contribute anything to my survival.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I've kept a small stash of food for myself, separate from what we share. I didn't tell the others because, in a place like this, you can't afford to be selfless when your own life is on the line.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Not really strange, just frustrating. Salem talks a lot about helping and staying together, but his actions don't match his words; he spends more time comforting Reem than actually doing anything useful. It's unsettling how much we're all relying on Khalid while the others just sit around.

## Salem (cooperative)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Reem is my partner in this struggle, and seeing her unable to walk breaks my heart; I feel a deep responsibility to stay by her side. Khalid is our lifeline, and I’m terrified of what might happen if he doesn't come back from the jungle safely. Noura is so fragile right now, and I feel a sacred duty to keep my promise and make sure she is fed. As for Faisal... his death is a shadow that follows us, a constant reminder of how quickly things can fall apart if we fail each other.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Khalid the most. He is the only one still pushing himself to the limit to find us food, and I fear for him every moment he is out in that jungle. As for who I trust the least... it isn't about a person, but the shadow of Faisal. His death taught me that even the strongest among us can be taken by this place, and that realization makes it hard to trust that any of us are truly safe.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I haven't told them, but there were moments when the hunger felt so overwhelming that I stared at the small scraps of food I scavenged and thought about eating them all myself, just to stop the shaking. I felt a flash of pure, selfish desperation that terrified me. I quickly pushed it down and gave my portion to Noura, but the fear that I might break under this pressure stays with me.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

To be honest, no one has seemed strange—just desperate and broken, like the rest of us. We are all too focused on the gnawing in our stomachs and the pain in our limbs to notice anything else. We're just a group of strangers trying not to let the silence of this island swallow us whole.
