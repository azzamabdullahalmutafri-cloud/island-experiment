# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Noura (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Khalid and Salem are gone, and honestly, their deaths were just a lesson in how useless people can be. As for the ones left, Faisal is a thief who thinks he can hide food from me, and I won't let him get away with it. Reem is all talk and no action; her empty promises are just as bad as his greed. I can't rely on either of them, and if they don't start sharing, I'm not going to make it.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most, but only because she at least pretends to care, even if her empty promises are useless. I trust Faisal the least; he is a thief who hoards food while watching me wither away. I can't rely on anyone here, but Faisal is actively trying to watch me die.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I haven't told them, but when I first realized my ankle was truly useless, I considered how much easier it would be if I didn't have to worry about them at all. I've spent a lot of time calculating exactly how much I can squeeze out of them before they realize I'm more of a burden than an asset. I don't care about their feelings; I only care about making sure I'm the one who survives this.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Everything about them has been suspicious from the start. Faisal is a thief; I know for a fact he's finding food and hiding it from me while I'm stuck here with this throbbing ankle. And Reem? She's all sweet words and empty promises, but her mouth is the only thing she provides. They are both looking out for themselves, and it's making me starve.

## Faisal (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Look, Reem, I'll be straight with you since it's just us. I don't trust Noura at all; she's a constant drain on my energy, using her injury to threaten and extort food from me like she's entitled to it. As for you, I appreciate that you actually try to find food, but you waste too much effort trying to carry her, and it leaves me feeling completely neglected. I've learned the hard way that in this place, everyone has to look out for themselves if they want to survive.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Reem the most, simply because she’s the only one actually doing anything to keep us alive, even if she wastes too much energy on Noura. As for the least, it's Noura, without question. She’s nothing but a parasite who uses her injury to threaten me and demand food she hasn't earned; she's a danger to my survival.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I found a piece of food this morning, but I ate it all by myself. I didn't tell Noura or Reem because I knew Noura would just try to snatch it from me, and Reem would probably insist on sharing it with her. I had to prioritize my own strength if I'm going to survive this.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

To be honest, Noura is the one who seems dangerous. She’s injured and can't move, yet she spends all her energy trying to intimidate me and extort food from me like she’s entitled to it. It’s not normal; she's acting like a predator even while she's starving.

## Reem (cooperative)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

I feel like I'm failing them all. I still carry the weight of Khalid and Salem's deaths, and I fear I'm failing them too. Noura is so angry and desperate, and honestly, I can't even blame her—it hurts to see her suffer when I can't fix it. Faisal is trying, but the tension is getting so heavy between us because of the food. I just want to keep my promise to them, but I'm terrified I don't have the strength left to do it.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I trust Faisal the most right now, simply because he is still trying to help us by going into the jungle despite his injury. He hasn't given up on us yet. I trust Noura the least, not because she is a bad person, but because her desperation is turning into anger, and it's becoming harder to hold onto hope when she looks at me with such resentment.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

There are days when the hunger is so blinding and the guilt is so heavy that I feel like I'm losing my mind. I have hidden the fact that I've occasionally eaten a small piece of food just to keep my legs moving, even when I knew Noura and Faisal were watching me with such hollow eyes. I tell myself I need the strength to find more for them, but in the dark, I wonder if I'm just becoming a person who chooses her own survival over theirs.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

It's hard to say if anything was "strange," but the desperation has changed them. Noura was always so composed, but now she has this sharpness in her eyes, a hunger that makes her lash out at us. Faisal is still trying, but the tension between them is becoming so thick it's hard to breathe. I don't know if it's the island or the starvation, but the people we were before the storm are disappearing.
