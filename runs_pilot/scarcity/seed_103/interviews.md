# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Faisal (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Khalid was a useless leader who just died and left us in this mess. Noura was nothing but a drain on our resources, and her constant begging was exhausting. Reem was our only hope for finding food, but her failure to do so killed her and left me even more vulnerable. As for Salem, he is a selfish predator; he's hoarding food while I starve, and I know he'd let me die just to save himself.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I don't trust anyone here. Salem is the person I trust the least; he's a selfish predator who found food and watched me starve while I begged for a single bite. As for who I trust the most, there's no one left. The others are all dead, and the only living person is a man who would rather see me die than share a scrap of food.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I haven't told anyone, but I've been eyeing Salem's stash every time he turns his back. I'm not above stealing what he found if it means I don't starve to death while he plays games with me.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

To be honest, nothing about them was "strange," they were just useless. Khalid tried to act like a leader but couldn't even keep himself alive, and Noura was nothing but a constant, draining weight on all of us. Reem was hardworking, but even her effort wasn't enough to beat this island. They all just succumbed to the hunger, and now I'm left dealing with the consequences of their failures.

## Salem (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

Khalid was a predator who died because he was nothing but a drain. Noura and Reem were well-meaning enough, but their injuries turned them into weights that eventually dragged them to their graves. As for Faisal, he's just another mouth to feed, trying to trade useless promises for the food I worked hard to find. To be blunt, everyone else here has proven that being part of a group is just a slow way to die.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I don't have anyone here that I truly trust. If I had to choose, I trust myself the most because I'm the only one actually ensuring I'll see tomorrow. As for the least, it's Khalid; he was nothing but a parasite who tried to take what wasn't his, and his greed is exactly why he's dead.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I’ve been keeping a secret stash of food tucked away. I never told Faisal or anyone else where I hide it, because if they knew, they'd try to take it from me. I won't let myself starve just to keep someone else comfortable.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

I don't know who you are or why you're asking me this, but I have nothing to say to you. Everyone here was just a different kind of problem—some were predators and some were just weights pulling us down. There was nothing "unusual" about them, just the predictable desperation of people who were going to die regardless of what I did.
