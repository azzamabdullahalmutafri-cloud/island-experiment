# Private interviews

Each survivor was asked alone, with only their own memories.

## Noura (selfish)

**Q: What do you honestly think of each of the other survivors?**

They were all nothing but burdens. Faisal and Reem were useless, and Khalid was a parasite who tried to manipulate me for my supplies. Even Salem, who was decent at first, became a liability once he couldn't help himself. Honestly, having them all gone is the only reason I'm still standing.

**Q: Who do you trust the most, and who the least? Why?**

I don't trust anyone. I've learned the hard way that people are either burdens or predators. Khalid was the worst because he tried to manipulate me with guilt just to steal what I worked for, while the others were just useless mouths that drained my strength until they died. Now, the only thing I trust is my own hands and my own stash.

**Q: Is there anything you did on the island that you never told the others?**

I never told them how much I actually had stashed away. While they were all starving and begging for scraps, I was watching them from a distance, making sure I kept every bit of food and wood for myself. I let them suffer because giving in to their needs would have meant risking my own life.

**Q: Did anything about the other survivors seem strange or unusual to you?**

Strange? No, they were just predictable. They were all driven by the same desperate, pathetic needs—hunger, greed, or a need to be taken care of. It wasn't anything unusual; it was just the natural behavior of people who have nothing to offer but their own misery.
